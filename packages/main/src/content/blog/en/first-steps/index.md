Hello, traveler!

If you've arrived here, it's because the internet has taken you far, leading you to this corner where I live.
It's not much, but it's honest work, and truth be told, it's been years since I've maintained a personal blog
where I can freely pour out my thoughts and ideas. However, I don't want to make a boring blog. If you follow me,
you'll learn something about Linux, BSD, AI, and crypto in general.

An example of what you might see? Ideas like deploying infrastructure for AI step-by-step would be an interesting
project, one that, for example, I've already done for my personal use. And that's a simple idea of what you can
see in this space.

Later on, I'll create a space on Telegram and YouTube where I can share audiovisual knowledge and engage with
the community that follows me. But for now, these lines welcome you.
